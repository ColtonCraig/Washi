# Washi for Dia

Washi's paper look in Dia (or any Chromium browser):

- **A paper new tab:** a handwritten hello, the time, one search box (your browser's own search engine, or type an address), your apps as paper tiles, and a sticky note that's there on every new tab. The gear has the Washi look: light, dark or match your Mac, five papers each, the tape color, grain, Scrapbook and animations.
- **The Washi button** (puzzle menu › pin it): **Reader** puts the article on a sheet of Washi paper (⌥⇧R, esc to leave, Aa for size and font), **Snapshot** copies the page as a picture and saves it to Downloads, and **Note** keeps a note for that site.

It only reaches a page when you click its button or press ⌥⇧R, so Dia doesn't ask to "read and change everything on every site".

## Install

1. Unzip **Washi-for-Dia.zip** somewhere it can stay (Documents is fine; don't delete the folder afterwards).
2. In Dia, go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the **Washi for Dia** folder.
5. Open a new tab. If Dia asks whether to keep the extension's new tab page, say **Keep it**.
6. Pin the Washi button: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin next to Washi.

To update, replace the folder with the new one and click the ↻ on Washi's card in `chrome://extensions`.

Reader uses Mozilla's Readability (Apache-2.0, `vendor/`).
